# Surface

Patterns observed across the sites in the [library](library/README.md).

- **Superellipse corners.** Spotted in Prod uses `corner-shape: superellipse(1.25)`, Cuelume uses `squircle`, and Wellmade sets app icons at a 22.37% radius.
- **Hairline rings and bevels.** libraries.dev adds a 1px ring to its soft shadow. Rare UI builds an "Apple" bevel from three inset hairlines.
- **Fixed-grey hairlines.** Wellmade avoids alpha hairlines so card edges don't tint from the content behind them.
