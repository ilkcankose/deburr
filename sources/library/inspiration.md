# Inspiration

Galleries of shipped work to look at before designing.

## Mobbin
https://mobbin.com/ · web + iOS · freemium (Pro $10/mo yearly, Team $16/seat)

- **What:** 621,500+ screenshots of shipped apps and sites. You can browse by screen, flow, UI element or animation, and save into collections. It has an MCP server and an API.
- **Best for:** Benchmarking how real products handle a specific screen or flow (onboarding, paywall, checkout, settings).
- **Its design:**
  - Type: Saans with a Recital serif, tight tracking (−0.4 to −3px).
  - Color: a translucent "glass" grey ramp built with `color-mix`.
  - Motion: ease-out-expo curves `(.22,1,.36,1)` and `(.19,1,.22,1)`; reduced motion is handled everywhere.

## ScreensDesign
https://screensdesign.com/ · iOS · paid Pro (around $29/mo, price varies)

- **What:** Full screen recordings of about 2,800 top-grossing iOS apps, from first launch to purchase. Videos are split into chapters and shown with revenue and install estimates. Paywalls are labeled by type.
- **Best for:** Studying onboarding and paywall flows of apps that are proven to make money.
- **Its design:**
  - Type: Grifter display with Rethink Sans.
  - Color: a blue-to-violet gradient brand (`#3b6df6` → `#6a3fe8`) with colored glow shadows.
  - Shape and motion: lots of pills and a bouncy `(.34,1.56,.64,1)` curve.

## before.click
https://before.click/ · iOS · free

- **What:** About 410 iOS apps' App Store screenshot sets, plus paywalls, onboarding and icons. It also runs ASO "Insights" articles.
- **Best for:** Designing or critiquing App Store screenshots.
- **Its design:**
  - Navigation: slash-command style (`/search`, `/featured`).
  - Type: Marksy display, Inter, Caveat accents.
  - Color: pink accent `#fb64b6`, with playful mascot animations.

## Refero Styles
https://styles.refero.design/ · web · free

- **What:** 2,000+ real websites, each broken down into an AI-readable DESIGN.md. The breakdown covers palette with usage notes, type scale, spacing, radii, shadows and "never" rules, exportable as Tailwind v4, CSS variables or tokens. Every style gets a one-line poetic summary ("Typeset terminal on white paper").
- **Best for:** Setting a visual direction and handing it to a coding agent. It's also the closest existing product to what this pack is doing.
- **Its design:**
  - Type: Neue Montreal with a Kalice serif.
  - Color: cool, slightly blue-tinted greys (`#0d0f15`, `#6f7179`, `#dfe1e7`).

## Wellmade
https://wellmade.fyi/ · web + iOS · free

- **What:** A masonry wall of about 1,575 pieces of credited creative work (UI, app icons, X banners, motion), mostly sourced from X. Items carry extracted color swatches.
- **Best for:** A fresh look at current craft across UI, icons and branding.
- **Its design:**
  - Type: Schibsted Grotesk only.
  - Color: pure black and white, with every other tone derived through `color-mix`. Hairlines are fixed greys rather than alpha, so card edges never pick up the color underneath.
  - Six type "voices" where each weight has one meaning: 400 reads, 500 is pressable, 600 is the one big thing.
  - Details: `text-wrap: balance` on headings, and app icons at a 22.37% radius.

## siteinspire
https://www.siteinspire.com/ · web · free

- **What:** A long-running, hand-curated website showcase. Its biggest categories are Agencies (2,400+), Typographic (2,100+) and Portfolio (1,400+). You can filter by style, type, subject and platform.
- **Best for:** Typographic, portfolio and agency web references.
- **Not verified:** A bot checkpoint blocked the CSS, so its own design wasn't assessed.
