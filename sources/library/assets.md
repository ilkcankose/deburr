# Assets

Ready-made images, backgrounds and sounds.

## Backdrop
https://www.backdrop.love/packs · PNG, Figma plugin · paid ($19/pack, Pro $50/yr)

- **What:** 1,400+ backgrounds in about 75 packs (abstract, landscape, paint, space), 20 images per pack. They're made to sit behind UI mockups.
- **Best for:** Making portfolio and launch shots feel premium.
- **Its design:**
  - Color: warm paper and ink (`#1d1c1a`, `#fdfdfc`) with one orange-red (`#ff5429`).
  - Type: TASA Orbiter only.
  - Motion: one ease, `(.22,1,.36,1)`.

## Grainient
https://grainient.supply/ · image, video, web shader · paid ($149/yr, $299 lifetime)

- **What:** About 1,500 grainy, smooth, animated and AI backgrounds at 4K–12K, plus a shader gradient editor.
- **Best for:** Buying high-res hero backgrounds instead of making them.
- **Maker:** Basit A. Khan.

## Lummi
https://www.lummi.ai/ · web, Figma, Framer, Canva · freemium (Pro $10/mo)

- **What:** Royalty-free AI stock photos, illustrations and 3D from credited creators. Includes reframe, restyle, background removal and duotone tools.
- **Best for:** Placeholder or hero imagery that's more coherent than Unsplash.

## We Are Heroes resources
https://www.weareheroes.digital/resources · files · free (newsletter sign-up)

- **What:** Aurora backgrounds, 2,000+ grainy gradients, vector blobs and hand-drawn icons.
- **Note:** The live site failed TLS; this content comes from 2024 archive captures.

## Cuelume
https://cuelume.dev/ · JS, framework-free · free (MIT)

- **What:** 14 UI sounds (tap, toggle, open, success, error…) synthesized live with Web Audio, with no audio files. Four "materials" change the character. Pitch rises along lists, and fast typing gets lighter. 9 kB.
- **Best for:** Tasteful sound feedback on the web.
- **Its design:**
  - Color: warm paper (`#faf9f6`, `#121212`).
  - Visuals: ASCII-drawn waveforms.
  - Shape: `corner-shape: squircle`.
- **Maker:** Daniel White.
