# Color

Patterns observed across the sites in the [library](library/README.md).

- **One orange is common.** It appears in Spotted in Prod (`#ff6a00`), loading.dev (`#ef551a`), Rare UI (`#FC4C01`), Backdrop (`#ff5429`) and Shader Gradient.
- **Warm off-blacks instead of pure grey.** loading.dev uses `#141413`, Backdrop `#1d1c1a` and Bencho `#17181a`. Cuelume and Backdrop sit on warm paper grounds.
- **Derived tones.** Wellmade builds every tone from black and white with `color-mix`. Mobbin uses a translucent grey ramp.
- **Some use no brand color at all.** 60fps is strictly grayscale.
